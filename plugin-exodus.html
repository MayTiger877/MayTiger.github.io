Exodus Delay

A delay plugin where every repeat has its own character. Each wet delay line runs its own percentage of processing by the following effects: Gain, Pan, Distortion, Phaser, and Reverb, making every echo unique. Each delay repeat is its own instance with its own FX chain. The result is a delay that evolves, rhythmically and texturally, across its repeats.

Interface

Features

🔗

Per-Repeat FX Chain

Each delay line instance has its own independent effects chain: Gain, Pan, Distortion, Phaser, and Reverb, all fully customizable.

🎢

Gain and Pan

Control the volume and stereo position of each individual repeat independently from the others.

⚡

Distortion

4 different distortion algorithms for unique tonal characteristics. Drive and threshold controls.

🌀

Phaser

Phase modulation including 5 different algorithms for phaser motion. Rate, depth and frequency controls.

🌊

Reverb

A simple reverb effect for adding spatiality to individual delay repeats. Room size and damping controls.

⏱

General Delay Settings

Full control over global delay time, feedback, overall wet/dry mix, and sync to tempo.
